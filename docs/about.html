About Dentora

An evidence-grounded BDS study assistant

Dentora is being built for dental students who need more than a generic chatbot. It combines BDS-focused study modes with retrieval from dental learning material and source metadata.

What makes it different

  • Study Chat for exam-relevant explanations.
  • MCQ Mode for one-best-answer practice and reasoning.
  • Viva Mode for examiner-style oral revision.
  • OSCE Mode for structured clinical-station practice.
  • PDF Tutor for asking questions from a temporary uploaded PDF.
  • Retrieval-grounded answers with source/page information when relevant evidence is available.

Grounding does not mean infallible

Dentora uses retrieval and verification safeguards to reduce unsupported claims, but AI systems can still misunderstand a source or make mistakes. Source chips show retrieved evidence; students should verify high-stakes facts in the cited material.

Current status

Dentora is in a private beta. A prespecified 60-question technical validation is being prepared across Endodontics, Oral & Maxillofacial Surgery, Orthodontics, and Operative Dentistry. That validation has not yet been completed, so Dentora should not be described as a validated system.

The goal is curriculum-specific, citation-aware BDS revision — not replacement of textbooks, teachers, or clinical judgment.
